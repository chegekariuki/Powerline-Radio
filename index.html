Our Story

About Powerline Radio

Powerline Radio was founded with a single mission: to bring a flash of hope to listeners across Kenya and beyond. Through the power of Christian music, gospel teaching, and community-driven programming, we connect believers and seekers alike to the transforming message of faith.

Broadcasting from the heart of Kenya, our dedicated team of presenters, pastors, and volunteers work tirelessly to create programming that uplifts, encourages, and empowers listeners every single day.

  • 🎵
    Uplifting Music

    Carefully curated gospel and Christian music around the clock.

  • 📖
    Biblical Teaching

    In-depth Bible studies and devotionals with trusted pastors.

  • 🤝
    Community Connection

    Programmes that unite and serve the local and global community.
